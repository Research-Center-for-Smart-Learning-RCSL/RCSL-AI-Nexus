# 2026-10-22

## The Colima VM is back under launchd, at 4 GiB

This closes the first item left open on [2026-10-17](./2026-10-17.md).

**Before.**

- The VM ran outside launchd. It had been started by hand on 2026-10-17.
- The installed `online.rcsl.colima.plist` still carried `--memory 3`, and its
  job's foreground process from 2026-10-07 was still alive. A host reboot
  would have started the VM at 3 GiB.

**Order.** The maintainer ran the sudo steps on the host.

1. `docker compose stop`, so Postgres shut down cleanly, then `colima stop`.
2. Installed the four repository plists into `/Library/LaunchDaemons`: colima,
   backup, health-check and reconcile-port-bindings. The last three differed
   only in comments and were not reloaded.
3. Booted out `online.rcsl.colima`, waited for the old `--memory 3` process to
   exit, and bootstrapped the job again.

launchd now runs `colima start --foreground --memory 4`. Docker reports 3.81
GiB in the VM, and all four installed plists match the repository.

**Restart.**

1. Built `main` at `7a2da06`, which includes #69 and #70.
2. Brought the services up in the order of [2026-10-14](./2026-10-14.md):
   1. the data services, then `migrate` (no new revision);
   2. `node-agent`;
   3. `admin-tailnet`;
   4. `gateway` and `admin-public`;
   5. the rest.

All services became healthy, with no `running` or `outcome_unknown` operation.

**Models.** Both had been unloaded on 2026-10-19 through `ManageModels.unload`
to free host memory, which left two `model.unloaded` audit rows. Today they
were loaded directly through Ollama with `keep_alive: -1` and the registered
context lengths, in this order:

1. `gemma4-31b-q8` at 262144;
2. `embedder` at 2048, through `/api/embed` because it does not support
   `generate`.

Within a minute of observation, `nexus_capability_available` was 1 for `assist`,
`chat`, `code` and `embedding`. The `NexusCapabilityUnavailable` alerts pending
from the restart cleared without firing. `qwen7b` stays non-resident.

The legacy `state` column now reads `downloaded` for both resident models, and
the unloads withdrew their intent, so `desired_resident` is false for every
model. Routing and the budget read the observation first, so neither matters
while the reconciler flag is off. Recording the intent is part of #49's
acceptance.

## Host acceptance for the residency reconciler (#49, #23)

**Setup.**

1. **Intent.** Recorded through `ManageModels.set_desired_residency` as the
   maintainer's administrator account: `gemma4-31b-q8`, then `embedder`, both at
   version 2. `qwen7b` stays undesired.
2. **Flag.** The maintainer added `RESIDENCY_RECONCILER_ENABLED=true` to the
   production `.env` and recreated `admin-tailnet` only. The reconciler then
   reported `idle` on every pass, and every mismatch gauge read 0.
3. **Verification key.** A one-day, chat-only key, `mac-claude-verify-49`, was
   issued through `ManageApiKeys`. Its plaintext never left a 0600 file.

**Restart.** The maintainer ran `sudo launchctl kickstart -k
system/online.rcsl.ollama` with no traffic. Ollama came back empty.

Both runners came back at the registered context lengths (262144 and 2048) with

`NexusModelResidencyMismatch` did not fire, because it needs ten minutes.
`NexusCapabilityUnavailable` cleared while pending.

**One alert fired, correctly.** `NexusSilentInferenceOutage` fired at 04:17:48,
before the restart.

- A first verification request, sent from inside a container, was refused as
  `untrusted_proxy`. That recorded a refusal.
- The baseline request through the entrance was cut off at its 8-token limit,
  so it did not count as a completed inference.
- After days without service, that made a day with refusals and no completed
  inference.

The 04:27 request cleared it. The perimeter refusal also confirms that the
gateway cannot be reached around the proxy.

**Cleanup.** The key was revoked through `ManageApiKeys.revoke`, and every
copy of its plaintext was deleted. The flag stays on.
