# Attributions

Third-party work incorporated into this repository, beyond the dependencies
declared in `backend/pyproject.toml` and `frontend/package.json`.

## Uiverse.io

Two decorative elements are adapted from [Uiverse.io](https://uiverse.io/),
whose collection is MIT licensed. Attribution is not required by that licence;
it is here because the work is individual designers' and the request to credit
them is a reasonable one.

| Element | Original | Designer |
|---|---|---|
| Busy indicator (`.nexus-spinner`) | [serious-mule-65](https://uiverse.io/AHMED-MIT/serious-mule-65) | AHMED-MIT |
| Dot grid on the sign-in screens (`.nexus-dot-grid`) | [sweet-termite-95](https://uiverse.io/adilsarfraz02/sweet-termite-95) | adilsarfraz02 |

Both were adapted rather than copied. The originals carry literal colours,
which do not follow a theme into dark mode and put a colour decision somewhere
the design system cannot reach; both now read the theme's CSS variables. The
geometry and the motion are the originals' and are what made them worth using.

They are the only two elements taken from that collection, deliberately. It is
an archive of several thousand pieces by as many different authors, with no
shared design language and, being visual showcases, generally no focus states,
disabled states, or ARIA. That is fine for a stateless decoration and wrong for
the interactive surface of an administrative tool, which is why everything a
user actually operates is shadcn/ui on Base UI instead.

## shadcn/ui

Components under `frontend/src/components/ui/` are generated by
[shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/) (MIT) and then maintained in this
repository. Because they are copied in rather than installed, they do not
receive upstream fixes automatically; see
[`docs/architecture/security.md`](./docs/architecture/security.md) section 10.

## Ollama

`backend/app/adapters/tokenizer/gguf_token_counter/gemma4_renderer.py` is a
Python port of Ollama's Gemma 4 prompt renderer,
[`model/renderers/gemma4.go`](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/blob/v0.33.2/model/renderers/gemma4.go)
at v0.33.2, and follows its variant selection in
[`server/renderer_resolution.go`](https://github.com/ollama/ollama/blob/v0.33.2/server/renderer_resolution.go).
It is a port rather than an independent implementation on purpose: the token
counter has to render exactly the bytes the runtime evaluates, and is tested
